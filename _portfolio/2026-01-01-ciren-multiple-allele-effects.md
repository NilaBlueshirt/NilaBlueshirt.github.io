Cyberinfrastructure facilitator on a CIREN project with Michael Lynch that simulates how early RNA-like organisms evolve under mutation, natural selection, and genetic drift. The work translates the original C++ research code into Python, accelerates large-population simulations with PyTorch on GPUs, and validates results against the original models.
