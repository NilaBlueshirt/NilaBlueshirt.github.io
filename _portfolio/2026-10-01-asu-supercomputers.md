Benchmarking and testing tools for ASU's supercomputers. HPL runs on Sol's A100 GPUs back ASU's Top500 and Green500 rankings, with HPCG and HPL-MxP runs alongside. After each maintenance window, a ReFrame suite runs node-level and MPI checks across ASU's clusters, including the OSU Micro-Benchmarks, and flags the nodes that need fixing before the systems return to production.
